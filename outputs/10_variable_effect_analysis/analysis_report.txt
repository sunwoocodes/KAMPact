[5] Interpretation
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Feature effect is descriptive and represents the difference in group medians normalized by the baseline IQR-derived robust scale.
A large effect does not prove that the feature caused the alarm or fault.
Detected vs Any-Miss comparison is performed at event level to prevent long fault events from dominating the analysis.
Very short events without a generated 0.5s window cannot contribute to the 16-feature event comparison.
